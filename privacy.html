Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

Hermes Assistant is a private, self-hosted tool used only by its owner. It accesses the owner's own Gmail and Google Calendar through Google APIs to read, organize, and send email and to manage calendar events on the owner's instruction.

Data use

Google user data is processed only on the owner's own server. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Storage and deletion

OAuth tokens are stored locally on the owner's server. Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
